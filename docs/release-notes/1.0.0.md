The first public release.

- **Crits, planned** (Full mode): whether your next task will crit, and the honest ways to make it one, cheapest first. *Relaxed* mode keeps everything else and never mentions crits.
- **Your day** in the character's speech bubble: Dailies left, the time to the day change, a warning before the day change would take all your HP, streaks at risk, quest invitations and drops left.
- **Tasks, your class, repeating To-Dos, history** and the crit journal.
- **The Stable**: up to seven playgrounds of your own pets and mounts, also as a live wallpaper, a screen saver and a widget.
- **On the phone**, each off until you switch it on: an ongoing notification, a widget, a Quick Settings tile and Night gear.
- **Back up to a file** to move to a new phone (never holds your token or User ID).
- **Try it without an account**: the whole app on a made-up account.

**Levers it may suggest** (you can switch each off): buying your own custom rewards · selling food (+1 Gold), eggs (+3) and hatching potions (+2 to +5) · a Health Potion when hurt · class skills · spending a stat point on the stat you chose · swapping gear you own, one piece or your best set for the roll (with a button to put it back) · scoring a task you've already done, together with one other lever (you confirm you did it) · buying something you're saving for at the right moment · seasonal transformation items · logging a bad habit, only if it happened (off by default; you confirm it each time).

It never suggests creating tasks or rewards, padding checklists, unchecking and re-checking, day-change or time-zone tricks, or editing stats, and it never scores a task you haven't done. Every score, purchase and sale is a tap by you; the one automatic change is Night gear, if you switch it on.
