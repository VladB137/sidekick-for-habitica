Sidekick for Habitica

This phone’s Android System WebView is too old to run Sidekick. It has version ; the app needs 66 or newer.

With Google Play: update Android System WebView there, then open Sidekick again.

Open in Google Play

Without Google Play: install the phone’s system updates (Settings, System, System update), or update the WebView from the app store the phone came with (on Huawei, Huawei WebView in AppGallery). Aurora Store can also update Android System WebView without a Google account.

Open the phone’s app store

Google Play updates it on its own on most phones; a phone that was just reset may not have had the update yet. In a web browser, update the browser instead.
